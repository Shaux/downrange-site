Account deletion · com.downrange.analyzer

Delete your
account.

You only have an account if you turned on Sync, or started web access on a phone without Sync. Deleting it removes everything stored in it.

1. In the app

Sync account. Open Settings → Sync → Delete cloud account and confirm. This permanently deletes your synced data, your target photos in the cloud and your sign-in. Data stored on your phone is not affected.

Web access without Sync. Open Settings → Web → Stop web access and confirm. This deletes your web link, the copy of your library and your target photos on our server. Settings → Clear all data on the phone does the same. Data stored on your phone is not affected.

2. If you can't open the app

Email support@downrangeprecision.com from the email address you sign in to Sync with (your Google address, or the one you receive sign-in codes at), with the subject "Delete my Downrange account". We'll delete your account and its data and confirm by reply, within 30 days.

A web link has no email address of yours, so we can't match an email to it. It deletes itself 60 days after your last web sign-in, or 60 days after you started it if you never signed in on the web. If you need it gone sooner and still know the Support ID shown in the app's Settings, email it to us with the subject "Delete my Downrange web link" and we'll delete the link and confirm by reply, within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Your synced groups, loads, rifles and barrels, round log, range sessions, zero resets, saved analyses and target presets
  • Your target photos stored in the cloud
  • The dates of your free analysis days
  • Your sign-in record: the email address, and the Google identifier if you used Google, that we hold

For a web link, the same list applies to the copy of your library, plus the made-up sign-in record and the RevenueCat Support ID we kept with the link. For a Sync account that used the web app, it also includes the record that your account has used web access (its account ID and the times of your first code word and latest code-word sign-in) and any code word still waiting. Hashed IP addresses from wrong code words are not tied to your account and are removed automatically within two days.

Nothing else is kept on our servers afterwards. Data on your phone is not affected: use Settings → Clear all data, or uninstall the app, to remove it. Purchase records are held by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies.

4. In a web browser

SIGN OUT in the web app's Settings clears that browser's copy of your library and photos. It does not delete your account or the copy on our server: use the steps above for that. A code-word session leaves nothing of the web app in the browser's storage once its tab is closed.
