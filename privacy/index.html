Privacy policy

Your data
stays yours.

Effective 22 October 2026 App version 1.1.0 com.downrange.analyzer
In short

Downrange works fully offline, and by default your data stays on your device. Your photos, shot groups, loads and settings are stored on your phone. There is no advertising, no tracking and nothing is sold.

Sync is optional and off until you turn it on: it keeps a copy in your own account, which you sign in to with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email, so your data follows you across devices. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.

Crash reports are also optional and off until you turn them on: they send us the error when the app crashes, never your photos, groups or loads.

The web app is optional too. It is part of Pro and opens your library in a browser at app.downrangeprecision.com. You sign in with your Sync account, or with a one-time code word from your phone. If your phone has no Sync account, starting web access copies your library to our server so a browser can open it; stopping it deletes the copy. The web app sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

1.What Downrange is

Downrange is a target-analysis tool for shooting-sports practice. You photograph or manually mark a shot group, and the app computes group statistics (spread, dispersion, point-of-impact offset and related metrics) on the device. It is a personal record-keeping and analysis tool, intended for adults (see section 10).

2.What stays on your device

Unless you turn on Sync or start web access (section 14), everything you enter or photograph stays in your device's own storage: target photos, shot coordinates, group statistics, loads, rifles, sessions, notes, velocity readings and settings. Uninstalling the app removes it. A session file you explicitly export or share, and crash reports if you turn them on (section 3), are the only other ways data leaves the app.

4.How long we keep it

  • Sync data: until you delete your cloud account (see section 8), when it is removed from our servers. Records you delete in the app are kept as a small deletion marker (no content) so your other devices remove them too.
  • Web link: until you stop it, use Clear all data, or sign in to Sync; otherwise until 60 days after your last web sign-in, or after you started it if you never signed in on the web. A daily clean-up then deletes the account, the copy of your library and its photos.
  • Code words: a code works once and for 10 minutes. Its hash is removed by a daily clean-up, once the code is more than a day past its expiry.
  • Hashed IP addresses (wrong code words and web link starts): removed by a daily clean-up once they are more than a day old, so for two days at most.
  • In your browser (web app): until you sign out, or, for a code-word session, until you close the tab (12 hours at most). See section 13.
  • Crash reports: kept by Sentry for 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall.

5.Camera, photo library and notification access

Downrange requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose to add a target photo, not on launch. Photos are used to mark shots and derive group statistics and are stored on your device. They are uploaded only if you have turned on Sync or started web access. Declining access falls back to the manual grid.

Downrange asks for notification permission only when you turn on one of the optional reminders in Settings (a barrel clean reminder, a note when your free analysis days are used, or a weekly nudge to log your session), never on launch. Reminders are scheduled and shown by your phone; nothing about them is sent anywhere. If you decline the permission, the reminders stay off.

6.Subscriptions and purchases

Downrange offers an optional paid tier through Google Play's subscription system. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing and verified using RevenueCat. When the app starts it checks your subscription status, so RevenueCat receives an app-specific user identifier and purchase and subscription-status tokens from Google Play, whether or not you have subscribed. If you use Sync, that identifier is your account's, so your subscription follows you across devices. The web app is part of Pro, so when you use it our server also asks RevenueCat whether your account has Pro (section 14). Neither Google Play nor RevenueCat receives your target, group or load data. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

7.No sale, no advertising

We do not sell your personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and do not use it for advertising or profiling. Because of that, no "Do Not Sell or Share" link is needed under California's CCPA/CPRA or similar US state laws.

8.Your rights and controls

  • Access and portability: Settings → Export session saves your records (not your target photos) as a JSON file, and Import session loads such a file back, adding what is missing and replacing nothing. If you use Sync, you can also ask us for a copy of what is stored in your account.
  • Delete your cloud account and synced data: Settings → Sync → Delete cloud account. If you cannot open the app, use the account deletion page. Data on your device is not affected.
  • Stop syncing: pause Sync or sign out in Settings at any time.
  • Stop web access: Settings → Web → Stop web access deletes your web link and its copy of your library and photos on our server. Settings → Clear all data does the same. Data on your phone is not affected. The account deletion page lists the other ways, including the automatic deletion after 60 days. In the web app, SIGN OUT in Settings deletes this browser's copy (section 13).
  • Crash reports: turn them off in Settings and nothing more is sent. A report holds no account or email, so we usually cannot tell which reports are yours; they are deleted automatically when the retention period ends.
  • Other requests (correction, objection, a complaint to your data-protection authority): email us at the address below. If you are in the EU or UK you have these rights under the GDPR; California residents have equivalent rights under the CCPA/CPRA.

9.Service providers

We use these processors under data-processing agreements: Supabase (database, storage, authentication; EU region), RevenueCat (subscriptions, and the Pro check for the web app), Brevo (delivering one-time sign-in codes by email, EU), Sentry (crash reports, only if you turn them on; EU data region), Cloudflare (hosting this website and the web app, and forwarding email sent to us) and Google (Play Billing and Google sign-in). Each has its own sub-processors, listed in its own privacy documentation.

10.Children's privacy

Downrange is intended for people aged 18 or over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted at this address with a revised effective date. Any new kind of data collection will be off by default and will need your opt-in.

12.This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies; even the fonts are served from here. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which, like any web host, processes your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks. We do not receive or keep visitor logs.

Email to support@downrangeprecision.com is forwarded by Cloudflare to our mailbox at Google (Gmail). We use it only to answer you.

13.The web app

The web app at app.downrangeprecision.com opens your Downrange library in a browser, on a bigger screen. It is part of Pro and shows the library you keep in the Android app. You sign in with your Sync account (Google, or a code sent to your email) or with a code word from your phone (section 14). Pro is bought in the Android app, not on the web.

Who hosts it, and what the host sees. The web app is hosted by Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages), which, like any web host, processes your IP address and browser details to deliver it and protect it from attacks. We do not receive or keep Cloudflare's visitor logs; the only thing we keep about requests is described in section 14. Once you sign in, the app also talks to our Supabase project in the EU, for sign-in, sync and photos and for the checks in section 14. Like any server, each of them receives your IP address with a request. If you choose Continue with Google, your browser goes to Google to sign in and then returns; Google's own policy applies there.

No cookies, analytics or other companies. The web app sets no cookies, runs no analytics and no crash reports, and loads nothing from other companies: its scripts, fonts and images come from app.downrangeprecision.com, and your target photos from our Supabase storage.

What your browser keeps, and when it is wiped.

  • Signed in with Google or email: the browser keeps your sign-in session and a working copy of your library and settings in its local storage, and your target photos in its IndexedDB. They stay until you sign out. SIGN OUT, in the web app's Settings, ends the session and deletes all of it from this browser. If the web app finds a stored library that belongs to a different account than the one now signed in, it deletes that library before showing anything.
  • Signed in with a code word: the session and the library live only in that tab's session storage and in memory, and your target photos in memory only. Nothing is written to the browser's IndexedDB. Closing the tab ends the session. It also ends after 12 hours at most, and the web app then deletes what the tab holds.
  • Your theme: the web app remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme. That is not account data, so it stays after you sign out.

Your browser's own history and cache are outside the web app's control.

The Pro check. Pro is checked by our server, never by your browser. When you open the web app, every six hours while it is open, and when you return to its tab, the server asks RevenueCat whether your account has an active Pro entitlement (section 14). If it does not, the web app shows a page saying so and opens nothing. A check that cannot reach the server is not treated as "no".

We may do this because you asked for the service (contract); for the host's security logs, our legitimate interest is keeping the service available and safe.

14.Code words and web links

A code word is a one-time sign-in for the web app. In the Android app, Settings → Web → Get code word makes two ordinary words, such as "copper heron". You type them into the web app within 10 minutes. A code word works once and needs Pro; asking for a new one replaces any unused code.

What we store for a code word: a hash of the code (a one-way scramble, mixed with a secret that we hold), the account it belongs to, when it expires and whether it has been used. We do not keep the words themselves. The code is valid for 10 minutes; its hash is removed by a daily clean-up, once the code is more than a day past its expiry.

What we store to stop guessing. Code words are short, so we limit wrong guesses: at most five from one IP address in 10 minutes, and at most 30 from everyone in 10 minutes. Starting a web link is limited to five times an hour from one IP address. To count, we record the time and a hash of the IP address (mixed with the same secret) for each wrong, used or expired code word and each web link start. We never store the address itself. Rows older than a day are deleted by a daily clean-up, so they stay for two days at most. A hash is not the address, but we treat it as personal data. We may do this because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Web links, for a phone with no Sync account. Settings → Web → Start web access makes a web link. Nothing is sent before you tap it. Then:

  • Your phone sends us the RevenueCat app user ID it uses for Pro (the Support ID in Settings). We keep it with the link and use it only to ask RevenueCat whether you have Pro.
  • We make a private account for your phone. It has a made-up address, such as link-…@link.downrangeprecision.com, that never receives email. You give us no email address, name or password.
  • Your phone then syncs to that account as it would to a Sync account: your groups, loads, rifles and barrels, round log, sessions, zero resets, saved analyses, target presets and target photos, plus the dates of your free analysis days, are copied to our Supabase server in the EU. The only purpose is to let a browser open them. Changes go both ways while the link exists. Sample data is never synced.

The link and its copy are deleted when you tap Stop web access, when you use Clear all data on the phone (if the phone is offline then, the 60-day rule below does it), and when you sign in to Sync (the phone asks first). Otherwise a daily clean-up deletes them once 60 days have passed since your last web sign-in, or since you started the link if you never signed in on the web. Your library on the phone is not affected. Starting web access again makes a new link.

If you use Sync, a code word signs the browser in to your Sync account, and uploads nothing beyond what Sync already does. We keep one small record that your account has used web access: its account ID, when you first asked for a code word, and the time of your latest code-word sign-in. It is deleted with your cloud account.

The Pro check and RevenueCat. To check Pro for the web, our server asks RevenueCat whether a customer holds the Pro entitlement. For a Sync account the customer is your Sync account ID, the same identifier the app already sends RevenueCat (section 6). For a web link it is the RevenueCat app user ID your phone gave us. RevenueCat receives that identifier and our server's key, and answers whether Pro is active and until when. It does not receive your library, photos or other data of yours. The check runs when you ask for a code word or start a web link, when a code word is used, and as described in section 13.

We may do this because you asked for the service (contract), except for the hashed IP addresses above (legitimate interest).
