Free and Pro

Free to measure.
Pro to analyse.

Every group you mark is yours, free, with no limit. Pro is for the questions you ask across many groups: is the zero holding, where is the flat spot, is this barrel opening up?

Free

Measure and keep

  • Unlimited groups and photos, with full group statistics
  • 3 rifles with one barrel each, and 5 loads
  • Range sessions, round counts and the cleaning log
  • Trend of group size over time, for all your loads or one
  • JSON backup and import
  • Optional Sync across your phones (beta)
  • Every Pro analysis feature free on your first 3 days of use
Pro

Analyse over time

  • Full Trend: velocity, cold-bore, charge, seating, weather and barrel rounds, with box plots
  • Zero check, compare and saved analyses
  • The Window tab and composite groups
  • Per-shot velocity analysis
  • CSV and PNG exports
  • Unlimited rifles, barrels and loads

Monthly with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers, or yearly. Google Play shows the price in your currency. Signed in to Sync, Pro follows your account to another phone. Subscription terms

How the free days work

New users get every Pro analysis feature free on their first 3 days of use. A day counts when you save or correct a group, open Zero, Compare or Window, view a Pro trend, build a composite, or save a Compare or Window session. Reopening a saved Compare or Window session, looking at group size over time on the Trend tab, or opening the app without doing any of those doesn't use one up.

After the third day, the analysis features lock again unless you subscribe; the trend of group size over time stays free. Nothing you made disappears: every group, composite and saved analysis stays viewable, read-only.

Subscribing

  • Monthly, with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers, or yearly.
  • Google Play bills you and shows the price in your own currency before you confirm.
  • It renews automatically until you cancel in the Google Play Store app: Menu → Subscriptions. Uninstalling Downrange doesn't cancel it.
  • New phone or a reinstall? Sign in to Sync the same way (the same Google account, or the same email address) and Pro comes with you, or use Restore purchases at the bottom of Settings.
