Features

What Downrange does
with your groups.

Measuring one group is the start. These are the tools that turn a season of groups into answers about your loads, your barrels and your zero.

Worked examples on every page

Trend charts

Plot group size or velocity against charge, seating, weather or barrel rounds. Box plots, filters, one line per load.

Learn more →

Zero check

Pool every group since your last reset to see whether your zero is holding or drifting.

Learn more →

Rifles and barrels FREE

Round counts on the action, each barrel and since the last clean, kept through barrel swaps.

Learn more →

Compare and combine

Side-by-side stats with a test of whether the difference is real, plus composites that pool many groups.

Learn more →

Window

Find the flat run on a charge or seating ladder, with group size and velocity alongside.
